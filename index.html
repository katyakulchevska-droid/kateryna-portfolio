Indigo Mental Club

Mobile App for Kids & CRM

Indigo Math Abacus for kids is an engaging intellectual trainer, specifically designed to simultaneously develop both hemispheres of the brain, enhance focus, concentration, logical and creative thinking in children.

Client Indigo Mental Club
Type Mobile App + CRM
Platform iOS / Android
About the Client

Client

Indigo Mental Club, a product company with more than 8 years of experience, specialises in teaching children around the world. Thanks to its global presence, the company not only educates children but also opens schools around the world.
What Was Built

Product

A mobile application for learning mental arithmetic and other games for the development of mental skills — combining education, play and creativity in one place.
Goals & Scope

Business Requirements & Constraints

  • Increasing market share in mental arithmetic and game-based learning.
  • Improving brand recognition and reputation in the field of education for children.
  • Attract new users and retain existing audiences with innovative and engaging exercises.
Obstacles

Challenges

Little time to create the app flow and conduct research, lack of management. The goal was to ship a compelling MVP for launch — fast, engaging and polished.
Creative Process

Game Characters & Motion

I created engaging game characters in Figma that serve as primary guides in the learning process, helping children acquire knowledge in a fun and effective way. I also designed the basic animations to show how each element should react, then collaborated with a motion design specialist to bring full realism to the application — building the complete storyboard from scratch.
Outcome

Results

Our application is interesting and convenient. The unique design — which combines space elements and original concepts — makes it attractive and convenient for users. It promotes quality learning and development, making it easy and exciting for young users.
Mindy Gem — Ukrainian Startup

SaaS Platform

AI-based Requirements Management Tool. I worked on redesigning and improving the product's logic — making it more intuitive, visual, and effective for the whole development team.

Client Ukrainian Startup
Type SaaS / Web Platform
Focus Redesign + UX Logic
About the Client

Client

This small Ukrainian startup successfully develops complex documentation and cooperates with AI for cost-effective development and optimised use of the team's potential in the field of IT.
What Was Built

Product

Comprehensive documentation for cost-effective development and enhanced team capacity utilisation. The service automates writing requirements from scratch — a convenient tool for interaction with requirements at all stages of development for the whole team.
Goals & Scope

Business Requirements & Constraints

  • Redesign and Visual Update: Enhance visual appearance and user experience.
  • Improvement of Logic and Functionality: Evaluate and optimise interaction logic for increased productivity and usability.
  • Enhancing Interaction with Requirements: Improve the interface for effective interaction at different development stages.
  • Enhancing Interactivity: Add new interactive elements for a richer user experience and increased engagement.
Obstacles

Challenges

  • User Engagement: Actively involving users in innovations was crucial for successful implementation and reducing resistance to changes.
  • Testing and Validation: Intensive testing was necessary to identify potential errors and ensure correct operation of updates.
  • Lack of User Agreement: There was potential for negative reaction from existing users to changes in design and functionality.
Process

Interview, Moodboard & Concepts

Before initiating the redesign, managers conducted interviews with users and tasks were created based on their feedback. A moodboard was created — not to change everything, but to make it better, adding primary and accent colours to enhance visual appeal. Several concepts were developed at the start, where individual elements were refined separately. The request was to keep only the essentials and eliminate anything distracting.
Outcome

Results

The result is a balanced and refined design that reflects the key aspects of the project. The use of colours enhanced the visual appeal and created a clear emphasis on key elements. The web version has become more user-friendly and understandable, contributing to the productivity of the entire team thanks to improved UX/UI.
Arounda Agency / KMA Tech

KMA Agriculture

The Agriculture product by KMA Tech is an innovative system aimed at combating locusts and ensuring crop protection. Designed for the optimal use of resources and effective control over pests.

Client KMA Tech
Type Web + Mobile App
Domain AgriTech / B2B
About the Client

Client

KMA Tech — a product company bringing innovations to the most trending markets around the world in B2B, B2C, B2B2C, Governmental and Enterprise segments.
What Was Built

Product

An application designed to solve problems in the field — in particular the problem of locust infestation. The product brings together a diverse team of professionals working together to effectively control and eliminate locusts. The platform facilitates seamless interaction and coordination between team members, enhancing their collaborative efforts in locust control.
Goals & Scope

Business Requirements & Constraints

  • Multidisciplinary Team: The product should bring together a diverse team of experts for effective collaboration and joint combat against locusts.
  • Real-Time: The system should provide immediate response to situations and real-time coordination of team efforts.
  • Analytics and Monitoring: Functionality for analysing and monitoring the scale of the locust threat, enabling informed decision-making.
  • Interaction and Reporting: Convenient interaction between team members and a reporting system to track productivity and effectiveness.
  • Geographical Integration: Ready for use in various geographic zones where locust attacks may occur.
Obstacles

Challenges

  • Operational Response: Ensuring an immediate response to the emergence of the locust threat and timely coordination of team actions.
  • Accuracy of Analytics: Ensuring accuracy and reliability of threat analysis to make correct strategic decisions.
  • Data Volume: Processing a large amount of data arising during the monitoring and analysis of locust spread.
  • Communication: Effective communication between different geographical zones and a unified reporting system for the entire team.
Architecture

Information Architecture & App Flow

A concise version of the architecture was developed, specifically tailored for each individual in the team — allowing effective interaction and utilisation of the product, taking into account the unique needs and responsibilities of each team member. An MVP of the app flow was created, customised for each of the 5 personas.
Outcome

Results

As a result of implementing a concise architecture and application flow for each of the 5 personas, we achieved effective collaboration and increased productivity in combating the locust threat. The platform was delivered as both a web version (for managers) and a mobile application (for field team members).
SURE Universal

Remote Medical Service Portal

SURE Universal provides the most secure, end-to-end IoT home healthcare solution for virtual ward, remote diagnosis, monitoring and telehealth — perfectly tailored to patient needs.

Client SURE Universal
Type Web Portal / HealthTech
Focus Redesign + New Features
About the Client

Client

The SURE Care platform is a medical data collection management system that provides healthcare professionals with a fully automated system to remotely monitor patients' vital signs in real time.
What Was Built

Product

New design and creation of new functionality for the SURE Care Platform (web). The project involved both a visual overhaul and the introduction of new features to support European market expansion.
Goals & Scope

Business Requirements & Constraints

  • Get funding from European partners.
  • Launch of the updated design in Europe and America.
  • Attracting new partners and maintaining the existing audience.
Obstacles

Challenges

Adding new functionality without robust testing and research — balancing speed with quality to meet the business timeline for European expansion.
Process

Research, Analysis & Concepts

Conducted a thorough analysis of competitors and created new flows based on patients' problems and needs. Initially, the team's goal was just to freshen up the visuals — but several concepts were developed that tweaked the standard look with improved logic and visual support. The team agreed that these ideas made sense and took them forward for refinement.
Outcome

Results

The portal improved its visual appearance and became significantly more user-friendly. The company is actively looking forward to cooperation with partners from Europe, supported by a polished, modern design.
